How to use Work Insights & KPI Feedbacks

A short guide to the two modules that sit alongside your KPI measurements.

On this page Work Insights KPI Feedbacks

💡 Work Insights

A place to log effort and misses that no KPI number captures — covering for a teammate, staying late, a missed deadline — and credit whoever was actually involved.

Logging an entry

  1. Click ＋ Log work insight (top-right of the Work Insights page, or from a KPI's detail view to link it directly to that KPI).
  2. Pick a Type: Proactive for effort worth recognizing, or Missout for something that fell short — deadline slipped, a defect, a gap.
  3. Under Credit to, type @ and start typing a name to mention who was involved.
  4. Fill in What did you do? (a short title), Category, Details, and Impact (the outcome, in one line).
  5. Optionally link the entry to a KPI measurement, mark it as spanning several days, and choose who can see it.
  6. Click Save entry.
Credit to works like a mention, not a form field you must fill in.
  • Mention nobody → the entry is logged for you.
  • Mention someone else → only they are credited.
  • Mention yourself and someone else → you're both credited, on the same entry ("Bhavesh Bhimra & Dipak Suryawanshi").

Categories

The Category list changes depending on Type:

ProactiveMissout
Attendance & Punctuality, Team Support, Initiative / Idea, Extra Hours, Resource Saving, Process Improvement, Other Technical, Functional, Communication, Process, Timeline, Other

Reading the list

  • My team / Everyone / Mine tabs switch whose entries you're looking at.
  • A blue left edge on a row means it's unseen by the credited person's manager.
  • Click any row to open it, endorse it (👍), comment, or — if you're a manager/admin — highlight it with a star.
  • Who can see this on an entry: Everyone at Vitec is visible org-wide and can be endorsed/commented on; Just my manager keeps it private.

Editing

Only the person who logged an entry (or an admin) can edit it, and who's credited can never be changed afterwards — re-crediting someone is a bigger decision than fixing a typo. Once an entry has its first endorsement, its Type, Category and title lock too (an admin can still override).

📋 KPI Feedbacks

Where non-green KPIs get a root cause, an optional linked Jira ticket, and a trail showing whether the fix actually worked.

Finding what needs attention

  • Employees see their own KPIs grouped by KRA area (a "Team KPI Dashboard").
  • Managers / Admins / HR see a "Remediation Board" grouped by employee (your own KPIs listed first).
  • Filter with All Non-Green, ⚠️ Re-escalate Only (a previous fix didn't hold), or ✅ Improved.

Logging a remediation

  1. Click any KPI tile to open the Link Remediation panel.
  2. Enter a root cause explanation — this is required.
  3. Optionally add a Linked Jira Issue Key (format KEY-#####, e.g. CARIT-12345) if a ticket is tracking the fix.
  4. Click Save Remediation.

If a Jira ticket is linked, use Sync to pull its current status, or View in Jira ↗ to open it directly. Admins can correct or unlink a mistyped Jira key from the same panel.

What the badges mean

BadgeWhat it means
Feedback neededNo root cause has been logged yet for this KPI.
Root cause logged — no fix trackedA reason was given, but no Jira ticket is linked.
Resolved in Jira — awaiting verificationThe linked ticket is closed; the KPI hasn't been re-measured to confirm it stuck.
ImprovedA later measurement confirmed the fix worked.
Not improved — re-escalateThe fix didn't hold; a new feedback loop is needed.
Closed unfixed — re-escalateThe Jira ticket was closed without a real fix; needs re-escalating.

When a badge says re-escalate, open the KPI again and log a fresh remediation the same way — the previous attempt stays visible for context.
